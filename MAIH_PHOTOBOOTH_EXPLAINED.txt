1. WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This website is a futuristic, sci-fi themed AI Photo Booth created for the 
Microsoft AI Innovators Hub (MAIH) community events, workshops, and college 
tech fests.

It allows attendees to:
1. Snap a photo of themselves using their smartphone or laptop camera.
2. Automatically generate an Ultra-HD branded event poster featuring their photo 
   inside a glowing neon cyberpunk frame with official community branding.
3. Instantly download the poster directly to their phone by scanning a QR code 
   (works on any phone over 4G/5G mobile data or Wi-Fi).


2. THE 3-STEP FLOW FOR GUESTS / ATTENDEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Using the photo booth takes less than 30 seconds:

  STEP 1: SETUP & CUSTOMIZATION
  - Displays the community logo and customizable Event Name (e.g., "AI SUMMIT 2026").
  - Lets you pick group size (1 to 4 people).
  - Organizer can optionally select a folder on their laptop to automatically 
    save high-resolution copies of every photo.

  STEP 2: CAMERA CAPTURE
  - Works with laptop webcams as well as mobile front/selfie and back cameras.
  - On phones, tap "Flip Camera" to easily toggle between front and rear cameras.
  - Features an intuitive 3-second countdown timer with visual flash so everyone 
    is smiling and ready.
  - Also includes a "Upload Photo" button if someone wants to use a picture 
    already saved in their phone's gallery.

  STEP 3: POSTER GENERATION & QR DOWNLOAD
  - The photo is instantly rendered into an Ultra-HD 2K poster (1446 x 2048 px).
  - The attendee sees their live poster preview.
  - A QR code appears on screen. When scanned with any iPhone or Android camera, 
    the poster downloads directly into their phone's downloads / photo roll!
  - Attendees can also tap "Download PNG" on laptop or "Save / Share" on phone.


3. KEY FEATURES EXPLAINED IN SIMPLE WORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

✦ 1. WORKS ON ALL DEVICES (LAPTOPS, TABLETS & SMARTPHONES)
     You can run the main booth on a laptop or large display screen, or attendees 
     can open the website on their own mobile phones. Clicking "📱 Open on Phone" 
     in the header shows a QR code that opens the full photo booth on any phone.

✦ 2. ULTRA-HD 2K CRISP POSTER QUALITY
     Unlike standard web photo booths that produce blurry images, this booth uses 
     a 2x high-resolution canvas engine (1446 x 2048 pixels) with proper hexagonal 
     face framing, neon glow accents, and razor-sharp text.

✦ 3. INSTANT 1-SCAN QR CODE DOWNLOAD (NO SAME WI-FI NEEDED!)
     Attendees do NOT need to connect to the organizer's Wi-Fi or hotspot. 
     The system generates a direct public download link. When an attendee points 
     their phone camera at the QR code, their poster downloads immediately over 
     normal 4G / 5G mobile data.

✦ 4. COMPLETE PRIVACY (GUEST VS. ADMIN PROTECTION)
     - GUESTS (ATTENDEES):
       Guests can only take their own photo and download their own poster. 
       All admin buttons, photo counters, and photo galleries are hidden from guests. 
       Attendees can never see or access anyone else's photos.
     - ORGANIZER (ADMIN):
       Only you have access to view all photos. Protected by a secret PIN (maih2026).
       When you log in, you get access to the Live Admin Gallery.

✦ 5. REAL-TIME ADMIN GALLERY & BATCH ZIP DOWNLOAD
     - Organizer can view all photos captured across all devices in real-time.
     - Fullscreen Lightbox: Tap any photo to inspect it in full HD resolution.
     - "📦 Download All (ZIP)": One-click button to download all attendee photos 
       as a single ZIP file for event recaps, social media posts, and reports.
     - Search & Delete tools to filter by time or remove test photos.

✦ 6. BEAUTIFUL TEAM PORTAL BACKDROP
     The landing page features a transparent community spotlight backdrop of the 
     MAIH organizing team that fades smoothly into the deep sci-fi navy background.

✦ 7. DUAL ARCHIVE (LOCAL DISK + CLOUD)
     - On the host laptop: Automatically archives all full-resolution PNG posters 
       into a dedicated folder named "AI Innovators Hub Posters".
     - In the cloud: Stores posters securely in Netlify Blobs so attendees can 
       download their posters from anywhere at any time.
